Embracing Small Things

Written by Johnoy Fowles | June 13, 2026

We are sometimes very passionate to see God’s work advance. Often, we consider doing a great task. With the advent of television and, more recently, social media, we are bombarded with great ministries. These ministries are doing big things: starting sanitariums, industries, and schools, and thriving health-food businesses and missionary initiatives that take them around the globe. But then, you consider your life, maybe stuck in your local church serving as an usher. And you think, “I want to do something amazing”, you feel you have much more to offer. Not that there is anything wrong with the desire to serve God in some amazing ministry, but working in obscurity may be a blessing you are not noticing. The Bible puts it this way, “He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much: and he that is unjust in the least is unjust also in much”*. Let's not underestimate the value of little things. “There are few who realize the influence of the little things of life upon the development of character.”(Patriarchs & Prophets, p. 222,223). Whatever you are doing now may seem insignificant. But embracing your task is an opportunity to develop your character. Do it with all your heart as if you are serving Jesus Himself! This mindset will be advantageous as God begins to direct your life as His providence shall direct. “Many are troubled because they are not working directly for the advancement of God’s Kingdom.. But the humblest work must not be ignored. If it is honest work, it is a blessing and may lead to the higher parts of the work.”* Embrace the small things as God prepares you for bigger things. Trust me, you will find peace in embracing the small things. Don’t put off your peace of mind.

God Bless!
