The Courage to Trust

Written by Eboneek Barrett | July 1, 2026

3 minute Read

One of the most comforting truths about God is that He sees the entire picture while we only see a small part of it. We often judge our circumstances based on what is happening today, but God is working with eternity in mind. He knows what is ahead, what needs to be developed within us, and how every detail of our lives can be used for His glory and our good. Scripture reminds us: "Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths." — Proverbs 3:5-6 (KJV) Notice that God does not ask us to understand everything. He asks us to trust Him. Often, our greatest struggle is wanting answers when God is calling us to faith. There have been seasons in my life when I could not see what God was doing. The circumstances around me seemed to contradict the promises I believed He had given me. Yet time and time again, He proved Himself faithful. Looking back, I can see that while I was worrying about the outcome, God was already preparing the way. The prophet Isaiah offers this beautiful encouragement: "Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee. Trust ye in the Lord for ever: for in the Lord Jehovah is everlasting strength." — Isaiah 26:3-4 (KJV) Peace does not come from having all the answers. It comes from keeping our focus on the One who does. Perhaps you are walking through a difficult season right now. Maybe the road ahead feels uncertain and your prayers seem unanswered. If so, remember that God's faithfulness is not determined by your circumstances. He is still working, even when you cannot see it. As believers, we can hold firmly to this promise: "And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose." — Romans 8:28 (KJV) Not some things. Not only the easy things, BUT All things. Even the painful seasons, the disappointments, the delays, and the unanswered questions can be woven into God's greater purpose for our lives. The courage to trust is NOT the absence of fear. It is the decision to keep believing despite it. It is choosing faith when doubt whispers louder and resting in the confidence that God is working all things together for our good and His glory.

A Challenge for You

Before you close this page, think back to a time when God brought you through something you thought you would never overcome. How did God resolve that situation? How did He make a way when there seemed to be none? How did He rescue you when you were on the verge of losing hope? Take a moment to remember how you felt when He came through for you. Remember the relief, the gratitude, the peace, and the renewed faith that followed. Now, identify one area of your life where you are struggling to trust God today. Perhaps it is your finances, your health, a relationship, a career decision, or an unanswered prayer. Write it down. Then spend a few minutes in prayer, intentionally surrendering that situation to God. Ask Him to help you trust His wisdom, His timing, and His plan, even when you cannot see the outcome. After you pray, take one practical step of obedience in the direction God is leading you, even if you cannot see the whole path ahead. Trust is not merely believing that God can act…it is choosing to follow Him while you wait for Him to act. The same God who carried you then is carrying you now. The same God who made a way then is able to make a way again. Trust Him with your next step and watch what He will do.
